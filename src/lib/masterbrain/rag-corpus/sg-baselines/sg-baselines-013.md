6. Modern SG Research and Data Sources (2022–2025)

6.1 Data Golf Integration for Talent Development

6.1.1 True Strokes-Gained Methodology Data Golf’s “true strokes-gained” framework addresses a
critical limitation of raw SG: field strength variation. Their approach estimates the relative skill level
of different tournament fields, enabling valid comparison across competitions.

Key finding for amateur application: In 2019 analysis, the same golfers gained 0.69 more strokes
per round against Korn Ferry Tour fields than PGA Tour fields—closely matching the estimated 0.72
stroke average player quality difference. This validates the adjustment methodology and demonstrates its necessity for multi-tour analysis.

Shot Scope proximity data by handicap and lie:

| Handicap | Fairway Proximity(50-100yd) | Rough Proximity(50-100yd) | GIR% |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Scratch | 26ft | 32ft | 59% |
| 10 | 41ft | 48ft | 32% |
| 20 | 49ft | 58ft | 14% |

The lie penalty (rough vs. fairway proximity) increases with handicap: +6 ft for scratch, +7 ft for 10-
handicap, +9 ft for 20-handicap, suggesting that lie difficulty compounds with skill limitation—a critical
interaction for AK Platform’s training recommendations.

The high variance inherent in golf performance—particularly putting—creates substantial sample size
requirements for reliable SG estimation. The AK Platform must implement data sufficiency flags to
prevent premature conclusions.
