2.4 Key Research Findings with Implementation Implications

2.4.1 Distance vs. Accuracy on Tee: Robustness and Conditions Broadie’s finding that carry
distance dominates fairway accuracy for SG:Off-the-Tee has proven robust across multiple datasets
and time periods. The estimated value of approximately +0.3 to +0.5 SG per round per 10 additional yards of carry distance reflects the baseline function’s steep slope at driving distances: a
280-yard drive leaves 140 yards (expected strokes ~2.95), while a 250-yard drive leaves 170 yards (expected
strokes ~3.08), generating 0.13 strokes gained from distance alone before accuracy effects.

Critical conditions for this finding:

| Condition | Effect on Distance Premium |
| --- | --- |
| Avoiding penalty areas/out of bounds | Essential-accuracy penalties in“dead” zones overwhelm distance gains |
| Rough not excessively punitive | On courses with U.S. Open-style rough, accuracy premium increases |
| Player can capitalize on shorter approach | Distance gain is realized through improved approach position, not tee shot itself |

The conditional nature of this finding has important implications for the AK Platform’s course-specific
SG adjustments: on narrow, penal courses (common in Norway), the distance-accuracy trade-off may
shift toward accuracy emphasis. The platform should implement conditional optimization rather than
universal distance-maximization recommendations.
